<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need crypto to play?" icon="gamepad">
    No. Classic is played with **NYX Points**, play money: every account starts with 20,000 and can claim a refill when it
    drops below 2,000 (once every few hours). NYX Points can never be turned into Credits or real money.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I sign in?" icon="right-to-bracket">
    With your email or Google account at [nyxeron.xyz](https://nyxeron.xyz). Signing in also gives you an embedded
    wallet on BNB Chain, so there is no extension to install. You need to be signed in to join live clubs.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I play with friends?" icon="users">
    Yes. A CEO opens their club live and shares the room code or an invite link. Friends walk in as **guests** and buy
    tokens at the bar, or take the decks as the **DJ**, and every player hears the same mix. See
    [Live multiplayer](/multiplayer-live).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if my connection drops?" icon="wifi">
    The game reconnects on its own. A guest's seat, with everything in it, is held for 60 seconds. If the host is
    away longer, the club closes, but the same room code can be reopened for up to an hour, so invite links keep
    working.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are the stocks real?" icon="chart-line">
    In Classic, no: the tokens you buy during a night go into a play portfolio for your score, priced from live
    quotes. In the City, yes: a purchase is a real unit of tokenized stock (bStock) held for you, where 1 unit is
    0.001 share. Today the City sells Tesla, as **TSLAB**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What are Credits?" icon="coins">
    The City's currency. Credits are created only when a USDC or USDT top-up (BEP-20) really arrives on BNB Chain:
    100 Credits = 1 USD. They pay for venue rent and real stock in the City. See
    [Real-money economy](/economy/real-money).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How much can I top up?" icon="wallet">
    At least 3 USD per top-up, up to 20 USD in any 24 hours and 100 USD in total. A small fee covers the gas the
    platform sponsors. Anything over a cap earns no Credits and is recorded for a refund.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I closed the tab during a top-up. Is my money lost?" icon="circle-question">
    No. The game reads every transfer to the platform's wallet from BNB Chain itself, including ones nobody reported,
    and credits each one once after BNB Chain confirms it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="When does the City open?" icon="city">
    Soon. The City runs on Credits only, so renting a venue, opening a shop, hosting a City party and buying real
    stock stay closed until top-ups are live. Classic and live clubs are open now.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do I follow updates?" icon="x-twitter">
    On X at [@nyxeron_xyz](https://x.com/nyxeron_xyz).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
