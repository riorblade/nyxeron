**Build your nightclub empire on BNB Chain.** NYXERON is a multiplayer nightclub management game where the drinks
on the menu are **tokenized stocks** — TSLA, NVDA, a US500 basket — tied to live quotes from BNB Chain's RWA market.

## The problem

Tokenized stocks are a real and growing asset class on BNB Chain, but most people only ever meet them as a trading
screen. NYXERON wraps them in something everyone already understands: **a night out**. You learn how prices move,
what a basket is and what a markup costs you by running a club, or partying in one, with friends, in real time.

| Role | You… |
| --- | --- |
| **CEO** | Rent a venue, stock the bar with tokenized stocks, set prices and house rules, book DJs, survive inspections and grow from a token booth to the Premium Venue. |
| **Guest** | Walk into a live club, buy tokens at the club's price and keep them in your portfolio. |
| **DJ** | Practise in the studio, then play a live set in someone's club — every player hears your mix. |
