## A day and a night

Every in-game day has two halves.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Day · run the business">
    As CEO you order stock from suppliers (Classic; in the City the platform holds the stock), set menu prices, hire staff, book a DJ or a band, promote the night, renew
    permits and set the house rules (cover charge, dress code, happy hour).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Night · 22:00 to 04:00">
    The club opens. The simulation runs minute by minute: guests arrive, dance, buy tokens, get rowdy; the DJ lifts
    the room; inspectors may walk in. You react live — promote, open the door, call security.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Report · the books">
    Sales, costs, tax and fines are settled, reputation moves, and the next day begins.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The rules behind each step are written out as formulas in [The night loop](/engine/night-loop).

## Roles

| Role | Goal | Key decisions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **CEO** | Grow from a token booth to the Premium Venue without going broke or getting shut down | Stock (Classic), prices, staff, talent, promotion, permits, house rules |
| **Guest** | Have a night out and leave with a portfolio | Which club, which tokens, at what price |
| **DJ** | Keep the room moving | Tracks, transitions, pads — live, heard by everyone in the club |

## Two modes, two currencies

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Classic · NYX Points">
    The single-player club career, played with **NYX Points (NP)**, a play currency that is never real money. You
    always start at Level 1 and climb level by level. AI-hosted events let you party solo.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="City · Credits">
    The shared city: CEOs rent venues, run shops and host parties for real players. A City CEO holds no stock: what the club or shop sells comes from the platform's warehouse. Everything is paid
    in **Credits**, which are minted only from USDC/USDT topped up and verified on BNB Chain, and the stock sold is real
    tokenized stock bought on-chain. See [Real-money economy](/economy/real-money).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
